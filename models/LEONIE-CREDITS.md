# Leonie Vesper — asset credits

## Anatomical head
MakeHuman Community bundled hm08 base mesh and female young/old macro targets.
Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman

License statement: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md
Full asset license is included as MAKEHUMAN-ASSETS-LICENSE.md.

The head was morphed, cropped, subdivided, fitted to the game rig, resculpted at the neck and given an original baked complexion. The final separate eyes, brows, clothing details, hair groom, bun and accessories were created for this character in Blender.

## Rig, animation and body components
The existing game's X Bot rig and clips are reused, as are authored hands, trousers and shoes from the Tomas development scene. Existing source asset rights continue to apply. This package does not assert ownership of the original game animation asset.

## Scene contents
The editable Blender file also retains the Blue character and hidden development references. Blue's Lee Perry-Smith reference head is CC BY 3.0; its credit and license are included. Exported leonie-vesper.glb contains only Leonie.

## Character interpretation
Based on npc/violet/soul.md: Leonie Vesper is a photograph restorer who checks sight lines and preserves evidence. Violet tailoring, an archive notebook, a capped retouching brush, muted leather shoes, and a restrained auburn low bun are visual interpretations of that biography.
