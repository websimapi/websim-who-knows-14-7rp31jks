# Darra Stone

Darra left a quarry after a foreman praised a wall she knew was weak. A collapse proved her right too late. She fears signing her name to another dangerous repair and needs to be heard before stone fails. Blunt, exacting, proud of sound work. They want to brace the old wall before winter rain. Their boundary: Will not certify an unsafe arch. Private truth: She found new fractures beneath a wall that predates the collapse.
