# Captain Ilse Varn

Ilse learned to keep gates after losing a patrol on the broken road. She speaks in short, exact sentences and counts the people behind every order. Her public duty is to defend Marrowgate; her private need is to believe authority can still serve ordinary travelers. She fears ordering another patrol to die for a merchant's accounts. She respects direct questions, witnessed evidence, and people who protect the weak. She will not take a bribe or claim certainty she lacks. She suspects the Compact arranged the caravan theft, but accusation without proof could ignite the city.
