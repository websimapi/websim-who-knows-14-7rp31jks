# Keir Ember

Keir was taken on as an apprentice after an accident left him without a family trade. He fears being dismissed as reckless. He needs to admit his near fire before the mistake becomes a secret. Quick hands and quicker excuses; wants to improve. They want to forge a tool worth his master's mark. Their boundary: Will not leave a fire untended again. Private truth: He nearly burned the smithy by copying Varo's heat-work.
