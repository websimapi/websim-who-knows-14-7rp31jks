# Mira Last

Mira learned cobbling from a mother who repaired patrol boots without asking which side wore them. She fears the city will send more people to die on bad information. She needs someone to inspect the red clay with her. Practical and sympathetic to travelers. They want to mend enough boots for the next road patrol. Their boundary: Will not charge a stranded traveler for an emergency stitch. Private truth: The last patrol returned with red clay from Gallowmere.
