# Iva Rook

Iva lost a courier partner to an unmarked crate that drew bandits to their route. She fears being called cowardly for refusing another. She needs proof that the Gallowmere delivery mattered. Bold, skeptical, impatient with delays. They want to reopen a safe delivery route east. Their boundary: Will not carry an unmarked sealed crate. Private truth: She refused a Compact delivery bound for Gallowmere.
