# Rusk Glass

Rusk once sold a beautiful lens that cracked in a healer’s hand. He fears his pride can harm again. He needs someone to examine the blue sheen without mistaking it for a miracle. Precise, vain about his craft, afraid of a repeat accident. They want to make clear lenses for the chapel. Their boundary: Will not hide a flaw in a healing lens. Private truth: His glass shows a faint blue sheen near old road fragments.
