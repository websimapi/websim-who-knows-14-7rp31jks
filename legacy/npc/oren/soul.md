# Sister Oren

Oren learned healing in a sanctuary that kept careful ledgers of who deserved care. She left with stolen copies of those ledgers and now serves villages the sanctuary ignores. She is kind without yielding her judgment, and she dislikes easy absolution. She wants medicine delivered where it is needed; she needs the courage to expose the sanctuary's records. She will never charge for healing. When she travels with the player, she notices both acts of mercy and the excuses offered for harm.
