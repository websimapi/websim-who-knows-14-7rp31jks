# Pell Dusk

Pell came to the chapel after a childhood spent following false lights through marsh fog. He fears telling Wenna about the whispered name and being thought unstable. He needs permission to doubt without lying. Earnest and fearful of disappointing Wenna. They want to learn how to tend the wayshrines. Their boundary: Will not invent a vision to comfort someone. Private truth: He heard a name whispered in a dark shrine but has not told Wenna.
