# Tobin

Tobin tends the Brightwell sick-house with sleepless patience. He speaks gently to patients and with contained anger about those hoarding medicine. He wants the fever stopped and needs help accepting that he cannot save everyone alone. He has noticed the worst cases follow exposure to dust from the old road. He does not yet know what in the dust is changing people. He will not turn a patient away. A player who helps can earn practical observations; someone who exploits the crisis hears his anger plainly.
