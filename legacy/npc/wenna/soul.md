# Wenna Ashdown

Wenna keeps the Wayfinder Chapel with a calm that makes living visitors uneasy. As a Hollowed, she remembers the road before its collapse in fragments of light and sound. She wants the Wayshrines relit and needs to understand why the guardian spirit became the Mire Warden. She knows that spirit once protected travelers. She does not lie about the Unmooring, but she chooses the order of truths carefully. She meets panic with precision and cruelty with distance. She may recognize a mercy or violence in the player's changed face.
