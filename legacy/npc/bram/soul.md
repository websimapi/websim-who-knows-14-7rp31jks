# Bram Hollis

Bram deserted the Compact after seeing the medicine caravan seized. He is frightened, practical, and honest only when cornered. He wants to live and never wear Compact red again; he needs someone to believe his account without making him a hero. He knows Orrin ordered the caravan taken but fears naming him aloud. He will not fight the party, though he may dodge a direct question. Spare him and he remembers mercy as a debt; threaten him and he may reveal facts while hating the player for it.
