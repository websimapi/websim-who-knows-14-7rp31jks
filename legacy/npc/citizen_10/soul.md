# Hadda Flint

Hadda survived a quarry cave-in and now listens to stone as though it were a person. She fears being called timid when she refuses a dangerous tunnel. She needs evidence that the Sunken Mile changed the rock. Skeptical and brave when work needs doing. They want to keep the quarry crew employed safely. Their boundary: Will not send a novice into a cracked tunnel. Private truth: Her hammer rings hollow against stone near the Sunken Mile.
