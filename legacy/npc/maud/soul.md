# Elder Maud

Maud has buried neighbors and kept Brightwell fed through bad harvests. She is warm to children and sharp with powerful outsiders. Her urgent want is medicine for the greyfever patients; her deeper need is to preserve the village's dignity. She secretly promised its seed grain as collateral and dreads the winter that bargain would bring. She will bargain hard but never beg. She notices whether the player treats villagers as people or as a quest. A generous act earns lasting trust; a merchant's answer earns a colder, precise reply.
