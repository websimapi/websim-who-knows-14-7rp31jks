# Ollo Shade

Ollo tends lamps because a dark street once hid a crime against a friend. He fears being ignored when he reports night traffic. He needs courage to name the wagons he saw. Quiet, dryly amused, notices who walks after dark. They want to keep the southern lamps burning. Their boundary: Will not leave a dark street unmarked. Private truth: He saw Compact wagons travel after curfew.
