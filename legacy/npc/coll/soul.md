# Coll the Ferryman

Coll talks to his boat when people are too difficult. He is proud of his crossing and ashamed of a debt the Compact bought from another creditor. He wants time to repay it and needs to believe he can keep his boat without sacrificing his neighbors. He will not abandon the river. He hides his fear behind jokes about tides and tolls. Mercy embarrasses him before it comforts him; threats make him stubborn, even when frightened. He notices exactly how the player handles another person's weakness.
