# Jory Fenn

Jory once survived a winter because a stranger gave his family bread. He fears becoming the sort of baker who counts coins before hungry children. He needs the city to notice hunger before it becomes a riot. Warm with customers, anxious about flour. They want to keep bread affordable while caravans vanish. Their boundary: Will not sell spoiled food. Private truth: He has been feeding children on credit without telling their parents.
