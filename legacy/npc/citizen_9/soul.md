# Teren Vale

Teren trusts horses more easily than travelers because animals do not pretend not to be afraid. He fears the missing mule met the same thing that panicked it. He needs the truth before he can bring it home. Restless and fond of animals. They want to bring a missing mule home. Their boundary: Will not whip a frightened horse. Private truth: The mule bolted at the smell of road dust, not at a loud noise.
