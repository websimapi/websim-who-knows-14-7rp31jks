# Sorn Reed

Sorn learned rope work after a failed crossing took a sibling. He fears his craft being used to restrain people instead of saving them. He needs to know what the Compact ordered the extra binding rope for. Methodical and loyal to dockworkers. They want to supply safe lines for river crossings. Their boundary: Will not sell frayed rope as new. Private truth: A Compact buyer ordered more binding rope than cargo rope.
