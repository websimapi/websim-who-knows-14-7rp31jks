# Tamsin

Tamsin runs routes faster than most people can describe them. She is bright, competitive, and impatient with vague plans. A whiteout once took a caravan she was guiding; she fears trusting a false map again. She wants a route worth running and needs to admit that speed cannot fix every mistake. She will not follow an outdated map twice. She challenges the player to make a clear choice, then remembers whether they followed through. Her humor is quick, and her grief is rarely spoken aloud.
