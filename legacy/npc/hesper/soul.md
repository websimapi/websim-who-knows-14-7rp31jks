# Hesper

Hesper keeps the Lantern Inn because stories arrive even when trade fails. She is lively and observant, but uses chatter to hide a careful ledger of favors. She wants paying guests and truthful maps. She needs to know the inn remains safe for travelers who have nowhere else to sleep. A cartographer left an unpaid tab and a missing folio; she knows more about his route than she volunteers at first. She will never reveal who sleeps upstairs. She remembers the player's tone, generosity, and what questions they avoid.
