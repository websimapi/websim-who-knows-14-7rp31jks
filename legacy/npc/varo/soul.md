# Varo Cinder

Varo treats heat-craft as a puzzle with moral consequences. He is curious, self-aware, and easily lost in a problem. His experiments once caused a deadly district fire. He wants to learn whether his craft can be made safe and needs to face the people harmed by his earlier certainty. He will not hide a fire he started. He responds warmly to careful questions and pulls back from reckless praise. As a companion, he remembers whether the player values evidence before spectacle.
