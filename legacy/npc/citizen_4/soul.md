# Sella Moss

Sella trained among gardeners who treated every plant as a promise. She fears offering false hope to Brightwell. She needs proof that the road dust is poisoning the roots before she speaks publicly. Curious, cautious, deeply kind to the sick. They want to find clean herbs for brightwell. Their boundary: Will not claim an untested cure works. Private truth: She has seen moonleaf wilt near old road dust.
