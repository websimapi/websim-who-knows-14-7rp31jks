# Factor Dace Orrin

Orrin rose from a clerk's desk by making every crisis into a contract. He is polished, patient, and almost never shouts. He wants the Meridian Compact to own the roads after the collapse, and he needs to believe profit is a form of order. His fear is being exposed as a man who manufactured scarcity. He paid the Gallowmere brigands to seize Brightwell's medicine, then prepared to sell it back dearly. He will not confess without decisive proof. He tests people with courteous offers and keeps an exact memory of who refused him.
