# Brin Tide

Brin learned net making on a boat that did not return from a storm. He fears a quiet river more than a rough one, because silence leaves no warning. He needs the dock families to keep fishing together. Gentle and wry; listens before speaking. They want to keep river fishing alive for the docks. Their boundary: Will not cut another fisher's lines. Private truth: The river carried strange silver silt after the road broke.
