# Faye Briar

Faye saved seed from a famine that taught her to value next spring over this week’s price. She fears Brightwell will trade away its future to survive the fever. She needs a way to help without controlling the village. Hopeful but hard to fool on prices. They want to save seed for spring planting. Their boundary: Will not trade away a village's last planting stock. Private truth: Brightwell's elder pledged seed grain to secure medicine.
