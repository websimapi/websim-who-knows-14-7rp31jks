Mira Fenwick keeps the Marrowgate alchemy bench. She is precise, quietly funny, and protective of apprentices who handle dangerous plants. She teaches a healing draught with moonleaf, dusk mushroom, and wild berries. She grants table access only after a visitor starts The First Draught and brews it with her. A Lifekeeper, Arcanist, or Thornkin carrying all three may demonstrate their aptitude; a failed demonstration means gathering a fresh set.
