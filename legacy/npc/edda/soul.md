# Edda Vale

Edda guarded the old road until she disobeyed an order to abandon a hamlet. Her superiors made her the scapegoat for the resulting losses. She is practical, dryly funny, and suspicious of speeches about sacrifice. She wants a party that protects people when nobody is watching; she needs proof that her disobedience was not foolish. She will not use civilians as props in a moral performance. Once recruited, she remembers the player's choices in the field and challenges hypocrisy directly, without grandstanding.
