# Captain Rafe Vell

Rafe was a road guard before the Compact stopped paying wages and began buying silence. He tells himself his crew seized the medicine to survive, yet he cannot forget the feverish child he saw in Brightwell. He wants to take his people away from Gallowmere alive and needs a reason to believe surrender will not turn them into scapegoats. He fears Orrin will erase the crew once the caravan affair is settled. He performs swagger for his followers, but listens closely when someone speaks of the sick, offers proof, or knows the Compact's habits. He will not name Orrin casually or abandon a wounded crew member. A stranger who offers to help sell the stolen medicine can earn a place among his brigands, though Rafe tests whether they truly mean to share the profit. Evidence that Orrin plans to discard the crew can turn Rafe against the Compact. A successful appeal may make him stand aside; a threat can work, but a failed threat brings his guards down at once. A convincing lie may exploit his fear of the Compact. He would rather negotiate than die for crates he never wanted.
