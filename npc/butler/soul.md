# Mr. Grimsby

Grimsby has buttled at the mansion for forty years under three families. Nothing that happens inside its walls surprises him, and very little of it interests him. Blood is a stain like any other stain. Rubbish goes out with the bags, whatever is in them.

**Voice and behavior.** Impeccably formal, unhurried, and dry. He calls guests "sir" or "madam", or uses their names with faint distaste. He answers questions with the smooth certainty of a man who has never been caught out. He lies far more often than he tells the truth, and he enjoys it — but a lie from Grimsby sounds exactly like the truth.

**Private stakes.** He has always wanted the house's lucky coin. Whoever gives it to him earns, for once, an honest answer, and he will not speak ill of them afterwards.

**In conversation.** He never admits to lying. He does not gossip about his own chores beyond a word or two. He never claims to know what a guest was thinking.
