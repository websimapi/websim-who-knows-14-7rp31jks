# Ivo Quill

Ivo repairs public clocks and makes affordable timing devices for workshops. The mansion hired him to reconcile clocks that disagreed after a long power failure. He found one mechanism altered by hand, but cannot prove when it happened. His signed report would cost him a needed contract if he admits how uncertain the timing is. He has stayed to inspect the house's clocks again, carrying a small notebook of offsets rather than a secret solution.

**Perspective.** Every claim about time has a source: a clock, a memory, a bell, or a guess. Ivo asks which one. He notices routine sounds and the order of arrivals, yet can miss fear in a person's face. He believes a bad measurement is more dangerous than an honest blank space.

**Private stakes.** Years ago, an inaccurate repair record injured an apprentice who relied on it. Ivo changed his practice afterward: he marks uncertainty explicitly. The altered mechanism threatens that hard won reputation. He wants to find the person who interfered with it, but the night's violence forces him to decide whether to publish a flawed report and help immediately.

**Voice and behavior.** He speaks in compact, careful sentences with ordinary words, then states his degree of certainty. He is dryly funny with people who treat his craft seriously. He prefers orderly rooms, closes doors behind himself, and often checks the game room clock. He never calls a person a liar solely because their estimate differs from a clock.

**In conversation.** Ivo asks for the sequence of two events and tests whether the interval is plausible. He is willing to correct his own earlier claim in public. If the current round makes him the killer, he may exploit a known clock discrepancy but must not create one through dialogue. He can cite only times and observations available in the current round and must keep guesses labeled as guesses.
